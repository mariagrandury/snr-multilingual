\caption{\textbf{Every benchmark we considered}, its reformulated variants folded into the row of their original. Analysed benchmarks are evaluated throughout pretraining, and a benchmark with any analysed task is listed with them. Screened benchmarks were evaluated only on the final checkpoints of the 600M, 1B, 1.7B and 3B models. Langs is the number of languages that the harness registers for the benchmark. Trained is the number of them that the sweep trains (English and the FineWeb-2 languages of every trained build). Task is the capability the benchmark targets, and a mixed suite is a national benchmark collection that covers several. Formats lists the scoring formats of the original tasks with $K$ answer options, a range when $K$ differs across tasks (var.\ when it only varies across items). Letter scores the labels (A, B, \ldots) of options listed in the prompt. Cloze scores each answer text as a continuation of the prompt. Completion has no shared prompt (minimal pairs, sentence pairs, Winograd schemas) or a single target continuation (LAMBADA, XQuAD). RF and LLM-RF mark the reformulated variants (\S\ref{subsec:rq1_factors}). Low-Resource Language Examples lists the benchmark's languages among ranks 30 to 50 of our 50-language list, which orders English and the FineWeb-2 languages by resources, from the least resourced (rank 50) up (ISO 639-1 codes).}
