\subsection{Decision accuracy across sizes}
\label{app:rq02_decision_accuracy}

\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\paragraph{Research Question.} \emph{Decision accuracy (DA), the framework's ground truth, is the probability that a benchmark orders a pair of models as an evaluation of larger models would (Heineman et al., 2025). Which benchmarks, in which languages, keep the ranking of the ladder's design variants across sizes (\textbf{DA-size}), along training (\textbf{DA-ckpt}), and early \emph{and} small at once (\textbf{DA-goal})?}

\paragraph{Setup.} We measure DA-size, the share of design pairs that a proxy's final checkpoint (90M--1B) orders like the 1.7B reference's. We use the seed-1904 runs of every language setting, ladder and data build. A pair differs on one design axis or several. The tasks are the benchmark tasks above chance at the proxy and at 1.7B with at least three comparable pairs, their bBPB twins (the gold answer's bits per byte on the same items, Figure~\ref{fig:app_rq02_bbpb}), and the per-language BPB of the 50 trained languages. A line divides matching by comparable decisions, summed over its tasks. We map DA per benchmark and language (Figure~\ref{fig:app_rq02_da_by_language}).
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\begin{rqfinding}
\textbf{1. A smaller fully trained model ranks the design variants close to chance.} On benchmark accuracy alone, DA-size is 0.51, 0.53, 0.52, 0.54 and 0.56 from 90M to 1B (mean over 359--553 gated tasks), and no proxy comes near the 0.90 threshold.
\\
\\
\textbf{2. The pooled line in the figure adds the bBPB twins at every size and is flat.} It reads 0.59, 0.57, 0.58, 0.58, 0.58 (90 \% jackknife $\pm$ 0.02--0.03) over 1175--1369 tasks, all 816 twins among them at every size.
\\
\\
\textbf{3. Bits per byte ranks like the reference and accuracy does not.} Per-language BPB reads 0.95, 0.95, 0.91, 0.73, 0.92 from 90M to 1B (mean over 50 per-language tasks) and \texttt{bpb\_macro} 0.94--0.97 (Figure~\ref{fig:app_rq02_bbpb_acc_gate}).
\end{rqfinding}
\end{minipage}
