\subsection{Above-chance items}
\label{app:rq12_above_chance_items}

\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\paragraph{Research Question.} \emph{If every benchmark-language task keeps only the items its 1.7B runs answer above chance, how much do decision accuracy, SNR, the above-random gate and the scaling fit improve, and does it matter whether the gate is applied before or after the items are chosen?}

\paragraph{Setup.} We measure what keeping only the items that the 1.7B runs answer above chance does to a task's SNR, signal and noise, and to the agreement of SNR with DA-size. We use the seed-1904 runs of every cell, ladder and data build, final checkpoints, proxies 90M--1B against the 1.7B reference. The tasks are every benchmark task in a trained language, with the above-chance gate applied before the item selection or recomputed on the kept items after it. The items are chosen with the reference's own answers, so the gains are an upper bound, not a held-out estimate. Decision accuracy is scored against the 1.7B ranking on the full task and on the kept items, and the gap between them is the part of the gain that comes from re-scoring the reference.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\begin{rqfinding}
\textbf{1. Keeping only the items the 1.7B runs answer above chance raises the SNR by lowering the noise, not by improving the ranking.} The median paired SNR ratio grows from $\times$1.27 at 90M to $\times$1.50 at 1.7B, because the k-fold noise falls ($\times$0.58--0.86) while the signal stays flat ($\times$0.86--1.07). Decision accuracy barely follows, even though the items are chosen with the reference it is scored against: the paired DA-size gain is +0.041 at 90M and +0.003 to +0.023 at 175M--1B (multi-axis pairs). (Figure~\ref{fig:app_rq12_above_chance_items}.)
\\
\\
\textbf{2. Fewer than half the items are kept.} The kept items are 45 \% of the items of the 507 tasks the committed gate passes at 1.7B, and 44 \% over all 840 tasks with an item above chance.
\\
\\
\textbf{3. Recomputed on the kept items, the gate passes almost every task.} It passes 816 of those 840 tasks at 1.7B and 83--90 \% of the 841 at the proxies, against 37 \% (90M) to 60 \% (1.7B) for the full benchmarks.
\end{rqfinding}
\end{minipage}
