\section*{AI use Statement}

Generative AI tools were used for code review, preliminary result visualization, spelling and grammar correction, formatting, and minor language editing of the manuscript. 
They were not used to conceive the methodology, design the experiments, interpret the results, or draft the scientific analysis. 
All AI-assisted content was reviewed by the authors, who take responsibility for the final manuscript and its scientific claims.

\section*{Limitations}

- even if we still pretrained a \nmodels model ladder, model interventions limited by compute cost

- even if we included up to 50 languages in the pretraining mixtures, the evaluation of the performance on many low-resource languages is limited by the availability of benchmarks covering them

- ladder sizes: our claims are limited not only by the availability of benchmarks but also by the capabilities of multilingual models at these sizes and the curse of multilinguality

- small models only: 90M--1.7B, with a 3B check on six configurations

- limitation of interventions? e.g., one tokenizer for every model and language

- English is half of every multilingual mixture, so only the share of the other languages varies

- single seed for most cells, and one 1.7B reference run per configuration

- many benchmarks are machine translated

- checkpoint noise is read in one window, the last 20\% of training during the cooldown

- ties between letter-format scores in bf16 at small sizes

- the model-based reformulation relies on a proprietary model

- contamination of the pretraining data with benchmark items is not checked

\section*{Ethical Statement}

- acknowledge high compute cost, but we hope this will help the community to take informed decisions, launch useful ablations and avoid wasting compute on noisy evals

- energy use and emissions of the sweep are not estimated

- low-resource languages have fewer benchmarks and less web data, which may not represent their speakers

- release of models trained on web data without safety tuning, for research use only
