1: # RQ4 — Surrogates: which cheap statistic predicts decision accuracy?
2: 
3: ## Research question
4: 
5: > Before training the reference, can a statistic computed on the proxy alone
6: > tell us that the proxy's decision will match the reference's? First among
7: > the 22 SNR definitions of rq03: which best correlates with **decision
8: > accuracy** (rq02) across the ladder's languages, and does the answer
9: > survive a change of training seed? Then beyond SNR (the paper's RQ3,
10: > [`documents/paper/sections/04_analysis.tex`](../../../../documents/paper/sections/04_analysis.tex)):
11: > its signal or noise part alone, the proxy's early-checkpoint agreement, how
12: > well its scores fit a scaling trend (rq01), its margin above chance (rq00).
13: > Finally, ~210 statistics from the literature and the AllenAI signal × noise grid (`catalogue.py`, `search.py`,
14: > [`literature.md`](literature.md)), per language, tier and benchmark.

24: ## Experimental setup
25: 
26: Outputs live under `pretraining/<pool>/` for the ladder pools: `predictivity`
27: (the plan grid, seed 1904 — the headline), `predictivity_seeds` (every seed;
28: replicates enter the signal pool as separate models), and the seed holdout
29: `predictivity_seeds_train` (seeds 64/313 at the ×3 cells) → `_test` (seed 1904
30: on the same cells). The signal population at a size is every design variant
31: trained there (language setting × depth × scheme); the noise is the
32: late-checkpoint std over the noise window, the k/20 points in the last 20 %
33: of the run — 80/85/90/95/100 % (rule 4) — (rq03
34: carries the seed-replicate noise). DA has two flavours: **DA-size**
35: (small→1.7B ranking, plus every other bucket pair) and **DA-ckpt**
36: (20/40/60/80 % → final within a size). The 22 variants are grouped into
37: families (dispersion / relative-spread / discrepancy / robust / depth) and
38: correlated with DA per language as Pearson r of log₁₀(SNR) vs DA; the
39: per-language BPB tasks (`bpb_<subset>`) take part like any benchmark.
40: The seed holdout is English-heavy by construction: among the ×3 cells
41: (L ∈ {1, 2, 50, 100}) a non-English harness task is only evaluated at L50 and
42: L100, so its per-language variant ranking rests on two language settings per
43: seed, while English and the BPB tasks cover all four.

45: ## Methodology
46: 
47: - **SNR per (task, size bucket)** is rq03's table (`snr_variants_per_task.csv`:
48:   22 variants, gated cells NaN, coverage per variant recorded there).
49: - **Decision accuracy** is joined from rq02 (`decision_acc_size_*`,
50:   `decision_acc_ckpt_*`; pair counts in `da_all_n_pairs_per_task_both_axes.csv`).
51: - **Ranking.** Per language, Pearson r of log₁₀(SNR) against DA over the
52:   (task, bucket) cells; the global variant is the highest mean r over
53:   languages and over both DA kinds (`top_variants_overall.csv`), and the
54:   per-language "most reliable benchmark" table reads that variant at the
55:   reference size.
56: - **Seed holdout.** The same per-language table on the replicate seeds
57:   (64/313) and on seed 1904 of the same cells; agreement is counted over
58:   the languages that have a best variant on both splits.

165: ### Statistics beyond SNR — setup
166: 
167: The truth is DA-size from [`rq02_decision_accuracy/`](../rq02_decision_accuracy/):
168: per task, the agreement between the ranking of the design variants at a proxy
169: size and at the target size, as carried in rq03's per-task table
170: (`snr_variants_per_task.csv`, `decision_acc_size_<proxy>`). The candidates are
171: read from the same table (`snr_*`, `signal_*`, `noise_*`, `decision_acc_ckpt_*`),
172: from rq00's above-random scores (margin above chance) and from rq01's log-N
173: fits (R²). The population per proxy size is the set of tasks with an SNR at
174: that size — the above-random survivors — so every candidate is scored on the
175: same tasks; benchmarks and per-language BPB are scored separately. Spearman ρ
176: between the candidate and DA-size over the population, per proxy size (the
177: `snr` block's `small_sizes` that have a DA-size column), with the p-value and
178: n; a cell needs at least 8 tasks; the noise part is inverted so that "higher
179: is better" holds for every candidate.

219: ### FineTasks' criteria — setup
220: 
221: A selection rule computed on one size, with no larger model in sight, is the
222: practical alternative to SNR: FineTasks (Kydlíček, Penedo et al., 2024)
223: selects tasks per language with four such statistics. `finetasks_criteria.py`
224: (moved here from rq09 on 2026-09-23; its outputs are
225: `pretraining/predictivity/finetasks_*`) computes them per (task, size) on the
226: `predictivity` pool — the 18 design variants at each size, grid seed, schemes
227: A/B, on the ten evaluated tenths — and judges them by DA-size against the
228: 1.7B reference, which the criteria never see. The gate is not applied (non-random
229: is one of the criteria under test) but the mask is carried; every candidate is
230: scored as Spearman ρ with DA-size across tasks, the way the SNR definitions
231: above are. Their "noise" is the spread across recipes at one step, which this
232: framework calls signal, so their SNR is the inverse of ours and the two should
233: not be expected to agree.

274: **Key findings**
275: 
276: - A selection rule that never sees a larger model recovers about a third of
277:   the chance-to-reference gap: the composite (monotonicity ≥ 0.5, cross-run
278:   SNR > 20, best margin > 3 std) passes 13 % of tasks at 175M and 38 % at 1B,
279:   close to our gate's 31 % and 49 %, and the passers read DA-size 0.52–0.54
280:   at every proxy against 0.45–0.47 for the tasks that fail. Their SNR
281:   criterion passes about 60 % of tasks at every size and does not
282:   discriminate; monotonicity is the size-sensitive one.
283: - As surrogates of DA-size the four statistics correlate at Spearman
284:   0.10–0.39 across tasks, the same range as our 22 SNR definitions (best
285:   `dispersion_shifted`, 0.28–0.38) and as rq01's ρ of score with size
286:   (0.15–0.38).
287: - Pooled over the four proxies (`finetasks_surrogates_rank.csv`) the best
288:   reference-free statistics are the task's item count (0.32), FineTasks' SNR
289:   (0.31), the proxy's own median DA-ckpt (0.28), `dispersion_shifted` (0.28)
290:   and the DA between the two rungs below the proxy (0.27); the gate reads
291:   0.15 and the ordering statistics 0.04–0.12; the cross-variant std of the
292:   final score, the relative noise and the relative signal correlate
293:   negatively (−0.27, −0.28, −0.23), so more spread across variants at the
294:   proxy goes with less agreement with the reference — against the
295:   framework's premise; rq02's own ρ, which needs the reference, reads 0.76.
296:   No reference-free statistic exceeds 0.33.
297: - rq02's two DA cuts move in opposite directions with size (panel d): the
298:   DA-size cut passes 12 % of tasks at 175M and 16 % at 1B, the DA-ckpt cut
299:   46 % at 175M falling to 19 % at 1.7B — the within-run persistence of
300:   [rq02 figure 7](../rq02_decision_accuracy/README.md#7-seed-uncertainty-and-the-da-ckpt-null)
301:   in another guise.
302: - Of their 96 picks, 45 exist in our registry; 49 % of the originals pass
303:   their own criteria on our ladder against 100 % of the cloze twins, with the
304:   same mean DA-size (0.56 against 0.57). The format choice they make
305:   implicitly (cloze for selection) is the one decision that moves the
306:   population; the thresholds move it little.
307: 
308: **Follow-ups**
309: 
310: - Bootstrap the ρ over tasks (a 90 % interval per statistic): the top
311:   candidates are within 0.05 of each other, and the seed holdout above says
312:   the exact ranking does not transfer — claim the family ("relative
313:   dispersion over checkpoint noise", or here "item count and persistence"),
314:   not a winner.
315: - The negative correlation of the cross-variant spread with DA-size deserves
316:   its own figure: signal at the proxy against the reference's agreement, per
317:   family, to see whether it is the twins (large spread, re-sorting with
318:   scale; [rq00 figure 3](../rq00_gate_and_curves/README.md#3-the-reformulated-twins-move-whole-families-across-the-gate))
319:   or general.
320: - A per-size and per-population version of the pooled ranking, as the `_b`
321:   panels do for the SNR definitions: the pooled Spearman mixes BPB with
322:   benchmarks and small with large proxies.

397: ## Surrogates from the literature — summary
398: 
399: `catalogue.py` computes 211 proxy-only statistics: the literature catalogue
400: ([`literature.md`](literature.md); every definition, its source and how our port
401: differs from it are in the paper's appendix,
402: `documents/paper/sections/app_snr_new.tex`), every AllenAI signal over every
403: noise (22 × 6, the k-fold benchmark noise included), and each signal and noise
404: alone. `search.py` scores them against 30 truths from rq02 (DA / τ-b / ρ for
405: DA-size, DA-goal and DA-ckpt, both pair sets, every pair or the pairs sharing
406: one L) on all the data: Spearman over one point per (benchmark, language)
407: cluster, permutation p below 30 clusters, Benjamini–Hochberg over every
408: configuration, and a split-half confirmation as an extra. The numbers are in
409: the generated block below; what they say:
410: 
411: - **The ceiling.** A truth re-read at 90 % keeps ρ = 0.66 (DA-size), 0.81
412:   (DA-goal) and 0.84 (DA-ckpt) with itself on the benchmarks.
413: - **No surrogate reaches it.** Over all benchmarks (239–245 clusters) the best
414:   are rank-stability statistics of the proxy's own run: window sign
415:   consistency for DA-ckpt (0.73), sign persistence for DA-goal (0.59) and the
416:   proxy's mean DA-ckpt for DA-size (0.57) — 73–87 % of the ceiling.
417: - **SNR is weak here.** AllenAI's `rel_std` / checkpoint noise reads 0.25 on
418:   DA-size (0.23 for relative dispersion, the paper's headline, against their
419:   R = 0.79). The k-fold benchmark noise beats the checkpoint noise for every
420:   signal (best ratios 0.48 / 0.51 / 0.63 on DA-size / goal / ckpt), but it is
421:   mostly the item count: `n_items` alone reads 0.51 on DA-size, and the best
422:   DA-size ratio, `dispersion_shifted / kfold_abs`, is the inverse noise
423:   (that signal is constant).
424: - **Dispersion alone points the wrong way.** Range, standard deviation and
425:   pairwise distance across variants correlate negatively with every truth
426:   (−0.20 to −0.35).
427: - **Particular cases** reach 0.94–1.00 on single languages or benchmarks
428:   (10–19 clusters) and on rq02's reliable subsets; the latter are selected on
429:   the truth, so their ρ is conditional on it.
430: - **Language count.** Over pairs of variants sharing L (L8, L15, L30) mean
431:   DA-size is 0.51 / 0.48 / 0.48: no trend, on a coarse test.
432: - **Split-half check.** 706 of 1,849 configurations chosen on one half hold on
433:   the other at q < 0.05.

583: - [ ] **The seed holdout ranks most tasks on a single model pair (open, 2026-09-17).**
584:       *What it is.* rq04 claims one SNR definition tracks decision accuracy
585:       best; the holdout (`rq03_noise_and_snr/compare_seed_splits.py`, reported
586:       in rq04's highlight and "Seed generalization" table) asks whether that
587:       ranking of definitions survives a change of seed: it is computed on
588:       `predictivity_seeds_train` (seeds 64, 313) and again on
589:       `predictivity_seeds_test` (seed 1904 of the same cells) and the two are
590:       compared.
591:       *The problem.* Replicate seeds exist only at 175M and 600M, so both
592:       splits hold those two sizes and DA-size there is 175M → 600M (the 1.7B
593:       reference never enters). English tasks and BPB rest on 15 model pairs.
594:       A non-English benchmark is trained only in the L50 cell, so its train
595:       split holds one pair — the two seeds of the same cell — and its
596:       "decision accuracy" is 0 or 1 and measures seed noise, not a ranking.
597:       The median task has one pair.
598:       *Implications.* The per-language agreement numbers (29 % / 57 %) and the
599:       low DA-ckpt ρ (0.07) are dominated by those one-pair tasks, so "the
600:       ranking does not survive a seed swap" may say more about the holdout
601:       than about the definitions; the DA-size ρ (0.75) leans on English + BPB.
602:       Nothing in the main `predictivity` tables is affected.
603:       *Options.* (A) keep only tasks with ≥ 6 pairs on both splits: an honest
604:       check, but English + BPB only. (B) treat replicate seeds as replicates,
605:       not as models: average them before ranking, and use the holdout only
606:       for the noise estimate — changes what the holdout means. (C) report the
607:       holdout for DA-ckpt only, whose pairs are checkpoints × variants within
608:       a size and do not shrink to one. (D) drop the holdout numbers from the
609:       highlight until more sizes have replicate seeds.
610:       *Recommendation.* A now (small change, honest scope, say "English and
611:       BPB" in the highlight), and revisit once the 90M / 1.7B-L15 cells and
612:       any further replicate seeds exist. Not implemented yet.
613: - [ ] Bootstrap CIs on per-language Pearson r and cross-pool Spearman ρ.
614: - [ ] Recommend a *family* (dispersion / relative-spread), not an exact variant
615:       — only the family transfers across seeds.
616: - [ ] Use a larger DA-size target (e.g. Apertus-8B) instead of the
617:       not-fully-converged 1B custom model.

634: ## External model-set tier (`all/external`, 36-sweep)
635: 
636: The `external` tier pools every non-custom model (reference HF, a06,
637: distillation, posttraining; sizes 270M…70B) with **no data-mixture axis**, so
638: "Signal" here is **cross-model dispersion** of final-checkpoint scores across the
639: external ladder — *how far different model families separate on a benchmark*,
640: rather than how far the three FineWeb mixtures separate. SNR magnitudes are
641: consequently an order larger than the custom pool's (e.g. `multiblimp_rus` SNR
642: ≈ 119) and DA is the within-family scaling-DA over that ladder. The headline —
643: **recommend the dispersion *family*, not an exact variant** — holds on this
644: disjoint model set, the strongest robustness check we have. Regenerate with
645: `python analysis/rq04_surrogates/snr_definition_postprocess.py --pool external`.

695: ### Highlighted result
696: 
697: - **Global-best SNR definition (`custom_swissai_hf`): `dist_std`** — mean Pearson r of log₁₀(SNR) vs decision accuracy **0.32** (DA-size), **0.43** (DA-ckpt), 0.38 overall. DA-ckpt is led by the mean-pairwise-distance / relative-spread cluster (`mpsd`/`rel_mpd`/`rel_mpsd` ≈ 0.51) — all dispersion-family, so recommend the *family*, not an exact variant.
698: - **Per-language anchor: `multiblimp`** — the highest-SNR above-random benchmark in **6 of 11** languages (`dist_std` SNR @ 1B).
699: - **Variant ranking generalizes across seeds under DA-ckpt** (holdout Spearman ρ **0.81**) **but not under DA-size** (ρ **-0.07**): the DA-size per-variant correlations are small and near-tied, so their ranking is noise-dominated and does not survive a seed swap — treat DA-size variant ranking as unreliable. The exact per-language argmax never transfers. **Never `tukey` / `projection`** (r ≤ 0).
