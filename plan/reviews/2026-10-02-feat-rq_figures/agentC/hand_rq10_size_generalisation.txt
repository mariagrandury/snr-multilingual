1: # RQ10 — Does a ranking that holds at 1.7B still hold one rung above it, at 3B?
2: 
3: ## Research question
4: 
5: Every other question stops at the reference: a proxy is judged by whether it
6: makes the decisions the 1.7B model makes (rule 10, `analysis/RULES.md`). The
7: 3B rung exists to ask the one question that cannot be asked inside that
8: frame — whether the reference itself is a proxy for the next rung. Four cells
9: were trained at 3B for it (deep, L ∈ {8, 15}, schemes A and B;
10: `plan/3b_models.md`), so this RQ is the only reader of
11: `build_snr_pool(above_reference=True)` and the only folder the rule-10 checker
12: exempts (`check_rules.EXEMPT`).
13: 
14: Snapshot: the ladder report of **2026-09-30 23:54** holds the four 3B cells'
15: evaluations (L8 and L15, schemes A and B, to their 145,200-step target), so
16: the 3B block below is filled in; the 3B L30 and L50 cells opened on
17: 2026-09-30 are still training and enter on a later pass. The driver
18: (`run_all_predictivity.sh`, last RQ block) reruns the step every pass.

20: ## Setup
21: 
22: - **Population.** Every family with a final at the reference rung; pairs at
23:   the grid seed, multi-axis and mono-axis (rule 15). With the four 3B
24:   families that is six multi-axis and four mono-axis pairs — above rule 5's
25:   minimum, but a per-task DA sits on a coarse lattice (k/6, k/4), so the
26:   pooled ratio over tasks is what the lines draw.
27: - **Gate.** `predictivity`'s mask at the proxy; at the reference rung the same
28:   one-sided 95 % Wilson rule computed on the reference's own runs
29:   (`above_random.scores_and_mask`), since the committed mask stops at 1.7B.
30: - **Comparison.** Panels (a) and (c) read the same families to 1.7B, so the
31:   two references differ in nothing but the reference. Today's preview
32:   (`--reference 1.7B --design 3B`) is exactly that comparison line on its own.
33: - **Known-answer check.** `--reference 1.7B --check` reproduces rq02's
34:   `decision_acc_size_<proxy>` per task for both pair sets
35:   (`predictivity_schemes/da_all_per_task_both_axes.csv`, the pool the rq02 decision figures
36:   pair over) — exact on all 3,312 cells on 2026-09-23. The script and rq02
37:   share the pair sets and the kernel; the `predictivity` folder's table is the
38:   A/B-only pool and differs by construction.
