1: # RQ7 — Does our SNR ranking agree with AllenAI DataDecide?
2: 
3: ## Research question
4: 
5: > Do the SNR variants and the "reliable benchmark" set we find on our ladder
6: > also hold on AllenAI's DataDecide / OLMo corpus, on the English benchmarks
7: > both share?
8: 
9: > ⚠️ **Only a handful of English benchmarks overlap.** On the ladder's auto
10: > task set they are `arc_easy`, `arc_challenge`, `hellaswag` and MMLU (via the
11: > Global-MMLU English split); the 36-sweep also shared `csqa`, `openbookqa`
12: > and `piqa`. With so small a universe, *set*-overlap metrics (top-K Jaccard)
13: > are uninformative — any K ≥ N spans the whole universe and is trivially 1.0.
14: > The real evidence is the **correlation of SNR over the shared tasks**
15: > (values: Pearson r; ranking: Spearman ρ), not the overlap.
16: 
23: 
24: ## Experimental setup
25: 
26: Outputs live under `pretraining/<pool>/` for the ladder pools (`predictivity`,
27: `predictivity_seeds`) and, as history, the 36-sweep tiers. For each pool we
28: compute, over the shared English tasks, the cross-corpus Pearson r of
29: log₁₀(SNR@1B) (values) and Spearman ρ (rank order), reporting the
30: best-correlating variant, plus the same at every matched size pair
31: (175M↔150M, 350M↔300M, 600M↔750M, 1B↔1B, and the unmatched 1.7B↔1B as the
32: last row of the size sweep). The headline stays the matched 1B↔1B pair: the
33: study's reference is 1.7B, but DataDecide stops at 1B and SNR grows with size,
34: so an unmatched headline would mix a size effect into the agreement. The AllenAI side is the
35: DataDecide ladder (25 data recipes at each size), so both corpora's "signal"
36: is a data-recipe dispersion at fixed size; on our ladder the recipes are the
37: language settings (and the scheme), which is a narrower axis — read a low
38: correlation as a difference in what the model population varies before
39: reading it as a failure of the SNR definition.
40: 
41: > ⚠️ **Methodological caveat — MMLU aliasing.** Apertus's
42: > `global_mmlu_full_en[_<subject>]` rows are aliased to AllenAI's
43: > `mmlu[_<subject>]` rows so the comparison can use the MMLU subjects, but
44: > **the two are not the same content**: Apertus runs the Cohere-Full
45: > translation/post-edit of MMLU (English split), AllenAI runs the original
46: > Hendrycks et al. MMLU. Question wording, post-edits, and sample coverage may
47: > differ. Plan: re-run the original `mmlu` lm-eval task on the multilingual
48: > Apertus checkpoints, then drop the alias and compare like-for-like. See
49: > `pretraining/<pool>/agreement.md` for the full caveat.

51: ## Methodology
52: 
53: - **Apertus side** (multi-seed, 3 mixes × 3 seeds × 4 sizes): the per-task SNR
54:   table from one of the seed-pool subdirs under
55:   [`../rq03_noise_and_snr/`](../rq03_noise_and_snr/). Each pool produces its own
56:   cross-corpus output (`pretraining/seeds_1904/`, `…/seeds_28_1797/`,
57:   `…/seeds_28_1797_1904/`, `…/custom_swissai_hf/`).
58: - **AllenAI side** (DataDecide ladder, 25 mixes × 5 ckpts at sizes
59:   150M / 300M / 750M / 1B): pulled once at build time and run through
60:   [build_allenai_variants.py](build_allenai_variants.py), which reuses every
61:   primitive from
62:   [run_apertus_snr_variants.py](../rq03_noise_and_snr/run_apertus_snr_variants.py)
63:   (`per_model_inputs`, `variant_signal_noise_snr`) plus
64:   `compute_size_decision_accuracy` from
65:   [compute_da.py](../rq02_decision_accuracy/compute_da.py) and the 22-aggregator
66:   `AGGREGATION_FUNCTIONS` list. The shared driver groups by `model` for the
67:   signal pool and by `model_family` (model name minus the size token) for DA — so
68:   neither corpus needs a `seed` column to contribute.
69: - **Task-name reconciliation.** Apertus ran only the multilingual
70:   `global_mmlu_full_en_<subject>` view of MMLU on the full ckpt-series; AllenAI
71:   uses the vanilla `mmlu_<subject>` names. We canonicalise the Apertus names via
72:   `global_mmlu_full_en[_<subj>] → mmlu[_<subj>]`. The parent-task filter on the
73:   Apertus variants CSV collapses MMLU subject facets into `mmlu`, so the
74:   post-alias shared set is the **7 standalone English tasks** (`arc_challenge`,
75:   `arc_easy`, `csqa`, `hellaswag`, `mmlu`, `openbookqa`, `piqa`). This aliasing
76:   is the headline caveat above — same task IDs, different MMLU content.
77: - **Correlation axis.** `log10(snr_<V>_1B)` on each corpus, Pearson r (values)
78:   and Spearman ρ (rank) over the 7 shared tasks; per-corpus top-K lists are
79:   ranked over this same universe.
80: - **Reference HF models skipped.** SmolLM3-3B / Olmo-3-7B / Apertus-8B each have a
81:   single training mix, so the data-mix-spread term in every SNR variant is
82:   undefined; including them would force the comparison onto raw `primary_score` (a
83:   capability number), conflating "task is reliable" with "task is easy".
84: 
85: **Which variant families transfer across corpora** (qualitative, stable across
86: pools):
87: 
88: | family | members | transfers? |
89: |---|---|---|
90: | **discrepancy** | `discrepancy`, `star_discrepancy`, `star_discrepancy_shifted`, `dispersion_shifted`, `gini` | **Yes** — top cross-corpus performers |
91: | **dispersion** | `dispersion`, `range`, `mpd`, `aad`, `rms_deviation`, `quartile_deviation`, `dist_std` | **Yes** — strong, especially single-seed |
92: | **relative-spread** | `rel_std`, `rel_mpd`, `rel_mpsd`, `iqr`, `rel_dispersion` | **No** — robust within-corpus, weak cross-corpus (includes AllenAI's default `rel_std`) |
93: | **depth** | `tukey`, `projection` | **No** — uncorrelated with DA in the first place |
94: 
95: **Enlarging the shared universe.** The 7-task overlap is the binding constraint;
96: AllenAI's `core` split has ~178 tasks Apertus does not yet evaluate. Highest-yield
97: additions, by category — adding them to the Apertus suite directly widens the
98: comparison surface:
99: 
100: | category | missing | how to add |
101: |---|---:|---|
102: | `mmlu_<subject>:mc` (multi-choice MMLU) | 53 | Apertus ran rank-classification (`global_mmlu_full_en_*`); add the `:mc` form. Roughly doubles MMLU coverage. |
103: | `mmlu_pro` (+ 14 categories) | 19 | In OLMES and lm-eval (`mmlu_pro` / `mmlu_pro_<category>`). |
104: | BBH (Big-Bench Hard) | 27 | `bbh_*` matches directly in lm-eval. |
105: | AGI Eval | 19 | `agi_eval_*` (OLMES) ↔ `agieval_*` (lm-eval). |
106: | Math | 14 | `gsm8k`, `gsm_plus`, `minerva_math_*` (lm-eval: `gsm8k`, `hendrycks_math_*`). |
107: | OLMES core knowledge / commonsense | 10 | `:mc` forms of boolq, openbookqa, piqa, commonsense_qa, socialiqa, winogrande, truthfulqa_mc1. |
108: | Generative QA | 8 | `drop`, `squad`, `triviaqa`, `medmcqa`, `jeopardy`. |
109: | `arc_*:mc`, `hellaswag:mc`, Code | 7 | Multi-choice ARC/HellaSwag; `codex_humaneval`, `mbpp` (need code sandboxing). |
110: 
111: **The harness tasks to evaluate on the ladder** are registered as the `allenai`
112: group of `configs/tasks.json` (lm-eval names, all rank-classification, all
113: scoreable by a sub-2B model): `arc_easy`, `arc_challenge`, `hellaswag`, `mmlu`
114: (the English original, which removes the Global-MMLU aliasing caveat), `piqa`,
115: `openbookqa`, `commonsense_qa` (DataDecide `csqa`), `social_iqa` (`socialiqa`),
116: `winogrande`, `boolq`, `medmcqa`. That is 11 shared task families (plus the 57
117: MMLU subjects) against 3 today. DataDecide's generative and code tasks
118: (`gsm8k`, `minerva_*`, `drop`, `squad`, `triviaqa`, `jeopardy`, `mbpp`,
119: `codex_humaneval`), BBH, AGI Eval and the `:mc` formats sit at the floor below
120: 2B parameters and would only add gated-out rows.
121: 
122: Not worth adding: `paloma_*` (perplexity, custom harness), `multitask_*` /
123: `custom_loss_*` (aggregates / loss probes), `copycolors:mc` (niche).

171: ## External model-set tier (`all/external`, 36-sweep)
172: 
173: The `external` tier (cross-model dispersion over the 270M…70B external ladder, no
174: mixture axis) compares its per-task SNR against the same AllenAI DataDecide SNR
175: table. Because the capable external models clear the above-random gate on **6** of
176: the 7 shared English tasks (vs the 4 the custom pool retains), the cross-corpus
177: correlation rests on a wider base — the strongest version of the agreement result.
178: Outputs in `all/external/`; regenerate with
179: `python analysis/rq07_external_frameworks/analyze.py --pool external`.

181: **Cross-corpus agreement over the shared English tasks** — Pearson r of log₁₀(SNR)
182: (values) and Spearman ρ (rank), best cross-corpus variant per model set. The same
183: discrepancy/dispersion family wins on every set; relative-spread (incl. AllenAI's
184: own `rel_std`) does not transfer:

186: | model set | best variant | Pearson r | Spearman ρ | n_shared |
187: |---|---|---|---|---|
188: | `seeds_28_1797_1904` (pure 3-seed) | `dispersion_shifted` | 0.98 | 1.00 | 4 |

210: ## Results from the 36-model sweep (2026-06, superseded)
211: 
212: The numbers below were generated on the 36-model sweep (4 sizes × 3 data mixtures × 3 seeds, 12 languages, pool `custom_swissai_hf` unless stated) and are kept as history; the predictivity ladder regenerates the blocks above.
213: 
214: ### Highlighted result
215: 
216: - **On the pure 3-seed pool (`seeds_28_1797_1904`) SNR values and rank order agree across corpora** — best variant `dispersion_shifted`, Pearson r of log₁₀(SNR) **0.98**, Spearman ρ **1.00**, but over only **4** shared English tasks after the above-random gate — near-saturated, so indicative rather than robust.
217: - **Seed-count trend is not robust** — Pearson r 0.90 → 1.00 → 0.98 (1 → 2 → 3 seeds) is over only ~4 shared tasks; with so few points the values saturate near 1.0 and don't form a reliable monotone trend.
218: - **Dispersion + discrepancy families transfer; relative-spread does not** — the cross-corpus winners are discrepancy/dispersion variants (`projection`, `dispersion_shifted`, `dispersion_shifted`), not the mean-normalised relative-spread family (incl. AllenAI's own `rel_std`).
219: - **Only 7 English tasks overlap the two corpora, and the above-random gate leaves just 4 of them** — so the evidence is the SNR *correlation* over that handful, not top-K Jaccard (trivially 1.0 on so small a universe). `custom_swissai_hf` keeps n_shared = **4**.
