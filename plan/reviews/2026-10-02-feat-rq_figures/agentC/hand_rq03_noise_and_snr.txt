1: # RQ3 — Noise and SNR: how noisy is a measurement, and what is its signal-to-noise ratio?
2: 
3: ## Research question
4: 
5: > How much does a measurement move when nothing but the seed or the
6: > checkpoint changes, how does that noise compare with the effect of a design
7: > decision, and what is each benchmark's signal-to-noise ratio under the 22
8: > candidate definitions? The per-task SNR table computed here is what rq04
9: > ranks against decision accuracy and what rq07 and rq09 read.
10: 
11: ## Experimental setup
12: 
13: Outputs live under `pretraining/<pool>/` for the ladder pools: `predictivity`
14: (the plan grid, seed 1904 — the headline), `predictivity_seeds` (every seed;
15: replicates enter the signal pool as separate models), and the seed holdout
16: `predictivity_seeds_train` (seeds 64/313 at the ×3 cells) → `_test` (seed 1904
17: on the same cells). The signal population at a size is every design variant
18: trained there (language setting × depth × scheme); the noise is the
19: checkpoint std over the one noise window of rule 4 (`utils.noise_checkpoints`:
20: the k/20 points in the last 20 % of the run, 80/85/90/95/100 %, the same rows for
21: BPB and benchmarks; `effect_vs_noise.py` below carries the seed-replicate
22: noise). DA has two flavours: **DA-size** (small→1.7B ranking, plus every
23: other bucket pair) and **DA-ckpt** (10 … 90 % → final within a size). The 22
24: variants are grouped into families (dispersion / relative-spread /
25: discrepancy / robust / depth); rq04 correlates them with DA per language. The
26: per-language BPB tasks (`bpb_<subset>`) take part like any benchmark, except
27: under the discrepancy family (below).
28: The seed holdout is English-heavy by construction: the ×3 cells are the deep
29: scheme-A runs at 175M and 600M for L ∈ {1, 2, 50}, and a non-English harness
30: task is only trained at L50 (Russian also at L2), so its per-language variant
31: ranking rests on one language setting per seed, while English and the BPB
32: tasks cover all three.

34: ## Methodology
35: 
36: - **Signal and noise per (task, size bucket).** Signal pool = every model at
37:   the bucket (`per_model_inputs`); each model's `data_score` is its final
38:   checkpoint, `step_noise` the std (ddof 0) over the noise window, the
39:   k/20 points in the last `noise_window` = 20 % of its run (80/85/90/95/100 %,
40:   rule 4 — before 2026-09-20 it was the last five checkpoints of whatever grid
41:   the task had, 80–100 % for BPB and 60–100 % for benchmarks, which put BPB's
42:   noise on a narrower window). The 22 aggregators in `snr/snr_variants.py`
43:   differ in how they turn the cross-model scores into a dispersion; all but
44:   `rel_std` share `noise = mean(step_noise) / mean(window means)`. Cells the
45:   rq00 gate marks at chance are NaN. The discrepancy family (`discrepancy`,
46:   `star_discrepancy`, `star_discrepancy_shifted`, `rel_star_discrepancy`)
47:   reads the scores as points of [0, 1] and returns finite but meaningless
48:   values on BPB and the loss, so the driver sets it to NaN on every task that
49:   is not a benchmark; `snr_variant_coverage.csv` records how many cells each
50:   variant covers per bucket, and the driver prints the ones that fall short.
51:   (Upstream's `signal_xlabel` / `noise_xlabel` strings are swapped; the
52:   definitions table writes them under the right name and no figure uses them.)
53: - **Effect vs noise.** For every (size, L, task) the intervention's |Δ| is put
54:   against the seed noise (sample std, n−1, over the seed replicates, where
55:   ≥ 2 seeds exist) and the checkpoint noise (std over the same 80/85/90/95/100 %
56:   window of the baseline cell, raw with n−1 and detrended with n−2 — under
57:   WSD the final window is still descending, so the raw std carries trend).
58:   Every std divides by its residual degrees of freedom. A ratio near 1 means
59:   the two levels are the same model as far as a ranking is concerned (the
60:   "read this against the seed row" rule of `ladder_report.md`); a decision on
61:   such a cell is a coin flip whatever its DA (rq05). Cells at chance at their
62:   size keep their row with no number (rule 1) and are grey in the panels.
63: - **Seed holdout.** `compare_seed_splits.py` builds the per-language variant
64:   ranking (rq04's Pearson table: at least `min_lang_tasks` = 3 distinct tasks
65:   per language, rule 8; `multi` is never a language, rule 7) on the replicate
66:   seeds (64/313 of the ×3 cells) and on seed 1904 of the same cells and writes
67:   `<train>__vs__<test>/headline_metrics.csv`; agreement is counted over the
68:   languages that have a best variant on both splits. Its README block below
69:   says whether the two pools hold the same cells. rq04 reports the numbers
70:   next to the ranking they test.

135: ## Preliminary findings (ladder snapshot, 2026-09-01)
136: 
137: Noise on the ≤ 600M ladder before any seed replicate existed
138: (`plan/status-09-01.md`, §5; checkpoint noise = std over the last 3 evaluated
139: checkpoints, median 0.004): the across-L range of final benchmark scores at
140: 600M has median 0.022 ≈ 4.4× that noise (56/60 benchmarks above 2×; top:
141: `xwinograd_en` 17×, `hellaswag_ru` 13×), and on BPB the separation is one to
142: two orders of magnitude above noise — the language-count axis gives genuinely
143: distinct models for SNR. The transformation table on the 14 September report
144: (18 seed pairs) puts the seed effect at Δ final loss −0.061 … +0.046,
145: Δ macro BPB −0.050 … +0.034 and Δ mean benchmark −0.004 … +0.007; the depth
146: effect on benchmarks is of the same order (−0.009 … +0.014 over the tasks each
147: pair shares), so
148: whether deep vs shallow is a distinct model for SNR is exactly what the
149: effect-vs-noise table above decides per task.

195: - [ ] **The seed holdout ranks most tasks on a single model pair (open, 2026-09-17).**
196:       *What it is.* rq04 claims one SNR definition tracks decision accuracy
197:       best; the holdout (`rq03_noise_and_snr/compare_seed_splits.py`, reported
198:       in rq04's highlight and "Seed generalization" table) asks whether that
199:       ranking of definitions survives a change of seed: it is computed on
200:       `predictivity_seeds_train` (seeds 64, 313) and again on
201:       `predictivity_seeds_test` (seed 1904 of the same cells) and the two are
202:       compared.
203:       *The problem.* Replicate seeds exist only at 175M and 600M, so both
204:       splits hold those two sizes and DA-size there is 175M → 600M (the 1.7B
205:       reference never enters). English tasks and BPB rest on 15 model pairs.
206:       A non-English benchmark is trained only in the L50 cell, so its train
207:       split holds one pair — the two seeds of the same cell — and its
208:       "decision accuracy" is 0 or 1 and measures seed noise, not a ranking.
209:       The median task has one pair.
210:       *Implications.* The per-language agreement numbers (0 % / 0 % at the
211:       family level on the 2026-09-30 tables; 29 % / 57 % when this was
212:       written) and the unstable DA-size ρ (−0.28 now, 0.75 then, against
213:       0.76 for DA-ckpt) are dominated by those one-pair tasks, so "the
214:       ranking does not survive a seed swap" may say more about the holdout
215:       than about the definitions.
216:       Nothing in the main `predictivity` tables is affected.
217:       *Options.* (A) keep only tasks with ≥ 6 pairs on both splits: an honest
218:       check, but English + BPB only. (B) treat replicate seeds as replicates,
219:       not as models: average them before ranking, and use the holdout only
220:       for the noise estimate — changes what the holdout means. (C) report the
221:       holdout for DA-ckpt only, whose pairs are checkpoints × variants within
222:       a size and do not shrink to one. (D) drop the holdout numbers from the
223:       highlight until more sizes have replicate seeds.
224:       *Recommendation.* A now (small change, honest scope, say "English and
225:       BPB" in the highlight), and revisit once the 90M / 1.7B-L15 cells and
226:       any further replicate seeds exist. Not implemented yet.
